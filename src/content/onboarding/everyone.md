## Day 1: know the rules

1. Read the [acceptable use policy](/policies/acceptable-use/) (5 minutes).
2. Learn the four data levels in [data classification](/policies/data-classification/).
3. Bookmark the [“Can I use AI for…?” guide](/faq/).

## Day 2: get access

- Sign in to the [Enterprise Chat Assistant](/tools/chat-assistant-enterprise/) with your corporate account.
- Never use personal or free accounts for work.

## Day 3: try it on something safe

- Summarise a public article or rewrite an email draft.
- Use the [prompt template](/library/prompt-template-structure/).

## Before the end of the week

- [ ] Attend the *AI Fundamentals* session in the [training calendar](/training/).
- [ ] Read how to [report an incident](/flows/incident-escalation/).
- [ ] Subscribe to [what's new](/news/).
