## Principles

1. **Use approved tools.** Only tools with status *Approved* (or *Restricted* with the required sign-off) listed in the [tool catalogue](/tools/) may process company information.
2. **Protect data.** Never enter data above the permitted classification for a tool. See [data classification](/policies/data-classification/).
3. **Stay accountable.** You own the result of any work you produce with AI. See [human review](/policies/human-review/).
4. **Be transparent.** Disclose AI assistance when a customer, regulator or contract requires it.

## Not allowed

- Using AI to impersonate people or generate deceptive content.
- Automating decisions that affect people (hiring, performance, credit) without an approved assessment.
- Circumventing technical controls or sharing licence credentials.

## Exceptions

Request exceptions through Information Security. Exceptions are time-limited and recorded.
