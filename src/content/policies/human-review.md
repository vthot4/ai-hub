## Requirement

Any AI output that leaves your team, is shipped to production or informs a decision must be reviewed by a person who understands the subject.

## Minimum review

- **Facts and figures** verified against a primary source.
- **Code** reviewed, tested and passed through CI like human-written code.
- **Legal or financial text** reviewed by the relevant function.
- **References** checked: models invent citations.

## Evidence

Keep the prompt, the tool and the reviewer's name when the output supports a material decision.
