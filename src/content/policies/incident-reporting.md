## Report within 24 hours when

- Restricted or confidential data was entered into an unapproved tool.
- An agent took an unintended action (deleted data, sent a message).
- A model produced harmful, biased or clearly wrong output that reached a customer.

## How

1. Stop using the tool for the task.
2. Email security@example.org with the tool name, time, and what data was involved.
3. Do not delete the conversation: it is evidence.

Reporting is blameless: early reports are always better than late ones.
